### RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, the Landlord is the lawful owner/authorized possessor of the residential property situated at **[PROPERTY ADDRESS]** (hereinafter referred to as the "Demised Premises"); and

**WHEREAS**, the Tenant has requested to take the Demised Premises on rent for residential purposes, and the Landlord has agreed to let out the same to the Tenant on the terms and conditions outlined hereunder.

---

### NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS MUTUALLY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

#### 1. TERM OF THE AGREEMENT
* The tenancy created under this Agreement shall be for a fixed period of **eleven (11) months**, commencing on **October 1, 2026**, and concluding on **August 31, 2027**, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.
* Any renewal or extension of this Agreement shall be subject to the mutual written consent of both Parties on mutually agreed terms.

#### 2. MONTHLY RENT
* The Tenant shall pay the Landlord a fixed monthly rent of **₹10,000 (Rupees Ten Thousand only)**.
* The monthly rent shall be payable on or before the **[DUE DATE]** of each English calendar month.

#### 3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
* The Tenant has deposited/agreed to deposit with the Landlord a sum of **₹30,000 (Rupees Thirty Thousand only)** as an interest-free refundable Security Deposit.
* The Security Deposit shall be refunded to the Tenant upon the peaceful and vacant handover of the Demised Premises, subject to deductions (if any) for unpaid rent, utility bills, or damages caused to the property beyond normal wear and tear.

#### 4. PERMITTED USE
* The Tenant shall use the Demised Premises strictly and exclusively for **residential purposes** for herself and her immediate family.
* The Tenant shall not use the premises or any part thereof for any commercial, illegal, hazardous, or unauthorized activities, nor shall the Tenant sublet, assign, or part with possession of the premises to any third party.

#### 5. UTILITY CHARGES
* In addition to the monthly rent, the Tenant shall regularly and punctually pay all **electricity charges** and **water charges** incurred during the term of tenancy based on actual consumption/meter readings.

#### 6. MAINTENANCE AND UPKEEP
* The Tenant shall maintain the Demised Premises in a clean, hygienic, tenantable, and proper condition throughout the term of this Agreement.
* The Tenant shall not cause any structural alterations, damage, or modifications to the Demised Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

#### 7. RIGHT OF ENTRY AND INSPECTION
* The Landlord or his authorized representative shall have the right to enter and inspect the condition of the Demised Premises by providing the Tenant with **reasonable prior notice**.

#### 8. TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
* Either Party may terminate this Agreement prior to the expiration of the 11-month term by serving **thirty (30) days' written notice** to the other Party.
* Upon termination or expiration of the lease period, the Tenant shall peacefully vacate and hand over the Demised Premises to the Landlord in its original condition, reasonable wear and tear excepted.
